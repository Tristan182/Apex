Sleep Protocol
Same wake time daily — your circadian clock is set by consistency, not duration. Even on weekends.
No screens 1hr before bed — blue light suppresses melatonin by up to 50%. Use Night Shift/warm lighting from 8pm.
Room temp 16–18°C — core body temp must drop to initiate sleep. Cooler room = faster onset + more deep sleep.
No caffeine after 2pm — caffeine's half-life is 5–7hrs. A 3pm coffee is 50% active at 9pm.
Morning sunlight within 1hr of waking — sets your melatonin timer 14–16hrs ahead. Improves night sleep quality significantly.
Magnesium glycinate at night — 200–400mg before bed reduces sleep latency and improves sleep quality.

iOS Steps Sync via Shortcuts
Apex can't read Apple Health directly from the browser, but you can set up a free iOS Shortcut to send your steps automatically.

Step 1 — Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone.
Step 2 — Tap + → Add Action → search "Health" → choose Find Health Samples. Set type to Steps, limit to 1, sort newest first.
Step 3 — Add a Get Numbers from Input action (to extract the count).
Step 4 — Add Open URLs action with URL:
apex://steps?count=[Number] (Replace [Number] with the Shortcut variable from Step 3.)

Step 5 — Set the Shortcut to run via Automation → Time of Day (e.g. 9pm daily).

Until the deep link handler is live, tap the ⟳ Sync button on the Health page to enter steps manually — or just type them in.

How your Life Score is calculated
Your Life Score (0–100) is a weighted average of six pillars, updated every time you log data:
Sleep — hours vs target (20%)
Steps — daily steps vs 10,000 goal (15%)
Nutrition — protein & calories vs targets (20%)
Gym — sessions this week vs target (15%)
Focus / Study — deep work hours logged (15%)
Finance — spending vs budget (15%)
A score above 80 means you're in the top tier for that day. Most optimised days land between 65–85.
